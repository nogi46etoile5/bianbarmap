📖 Mini Guide: WLW & Sisterhood

👩‍❤️‍👩 WLW (Women Loving Women)

An umbrella term for women who are attracted to women (lesbian, bisexual, queer, etc.). It refers to titles focusing on romantic relationships and intimacy between women.

🤝 Sisterhood

Refers to solidarity, deep emotional bonds, and mutual empowerment among women as they navigate shared struggles, social expectations, and personal growth.
